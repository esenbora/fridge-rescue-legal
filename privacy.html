What the app collects

DataWhyHow long we keep it

There is no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no sale of personal data. The app does not ask for your name, e-mail address, or phone number.

Photos and the AI provider

Scanning is optional and asks for your explicit permission first. When you allow it, the photo is uploaded to a private, user-scoped folder in Supabase Storage and sent to OpenAI, which reads only the visible ingredients. OpenAI does not write the recipes; those come from a fixed catalogue that a human has cooked and checked. After detection, the stored file is deleted. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Settings, and ingredient entry keeps working without any photo.

Processors we use

  • Supabase — anonymous authentication and temporary photo storage
  • OpenAI — ingredient detection from the photo you submit
  • RevenueCat — subscription and trial status
  • Apple — payment processing and app distribution

Write to buzzicra@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Fridge Rescue is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Fotoğraflar ve yapay zekâ sağlayıcısı
